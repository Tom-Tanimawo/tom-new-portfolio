I bring ideas to life with thoughtful interfaces, accessible patterns, and a focus on the people using them.

I’m Tom, a frontend developer based in Benin City, Nigeria. I enjoy taking a project from an early idea to a thoughtful, usable interface, with careful attention to structure, visual consistency, and the way each interaction feels.

My toolkit includes HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and responsive design. I value semantic markup, accessible patterns, maintainable components, and performance, and I’m always improving my process through practice and feedback. I’m keen to contribute to a team, collaborate with designers and developers, and build digital products that solve real needs.
